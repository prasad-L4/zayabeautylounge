THE STORY BEHIND THE BEAUTY

I am Lasitha, founder of Zaya Beauty Lounge, a makeup artist and educator with over 6 years of experience. What began as a childhood love for makeup has grown into a passion for creating beauty, confidence and meaningful experiences for every client.

FROM PASSION TO PURPOSE

I come from a small village in Alappuzha. After completing my BCA and MCA from STAS, Pathanamthitta, I chose to follow the passion I had carried since childhood. With the support of my parents, I stepped into the world of professional beauty and makeup.

My first guru was the National Award-winning makeup artist Shri Pattanam Rasheed Sir, who gave me the foundation of professional makeup. Since then, I have continued to learn through national and international training, growing as a makeup artist and educator.

I had opportunities to work with the cinema industry, but my dream was to create a beauty space of my own. That dream became Zaya — a place where I could offer world-class beauty services with quality, care and affordability.
